# Game detail and journey

Owned games share `Journey` in `src/components/journey.tsx`, whether opened from `/game` (current PSN game) or `/title/*`. A game not owned keeps the discovery anatomy in `src/pages/title.tsx` (`example.tsx`).

## Owned game: top to bottom

1. Existing immersive hero, catalog backdrop/scenes, name, platform and personal `HeroProgress`. `UnobtainableBanner` remains visible when appropriate.
2. The journey opens on the current pending guide suggestion (or the first pending trophy in guide order), inside a landscape using existing game artwork with a neutral fallback. A horizontal, keyboard-operable route uses the actual trophy artwork with an icon fallback and focuses on pending milestones across stages. Missable markers require an exact trophy match in the guide; hidden pending trophies keep their artwork and risk details protected. Platinum has a distinct destination marker with a subtle orbit disabled by reduced motion. The route fits whole milestones in the visible area and shifts through the previous/next controls, without a horizontal scrollbar; selection moves within each fixed visible page and only crosses pages at its boundaries; the trajectory has no visible stage divisions, and all earned trophies stay accessible in one history disclosure, with their artwork, full objective descriptions and a completion marker. Internal guide phases determine advisory ordering for games without reviewed knowledge. Requiem and Odyssey use edition-scoped reviewed models in `shared/journey/`: Requiem has 36 objectives with chapter preparation and recovery; Odyssey has 51 base objectives and parallel preparation, with unknown quest/level/inventory requirements explicitly checked instead of chapter-based recovery. Neither model infers current location from unlocks. Catalog previews carry the actual PSN edition separately from the chat catalog identifier. The same evaluator supplies server focus. A compact reviewed-context block exposes known versus unknown state and grouped preparation; partial or ambiguous lists do not claim personalized guidance. Selecting a milestone shows its advice in place without changing progress. Ongoing objectives remain visible independently of the selected milestone until PSN confirms completion. Eligibility uses conservative existing evidence: unique repeated guide references, a warning explicitly beginning with a campaign-wide duration, a PSN counter target greater than one, or an early tip naming the trophy uniquely. Hidden trophies, platinum and DLC are excluded. Missing progress stays unknown; a full counter alone never means earned. The start-preview toggle projects the same real list without personal earned states or counters, explicitly labels the comparison and never writes account data. Ambient animation respects reduced motion. A connected trophy roadmap uses existing guide phase order and unique exact localized-name matches. Repeated references appear once; unmatched trophies are explicitly unplaced, platinum is the final milestone and DLC remains separate. Earned state comes only from PSN. Existing generated guide tips and warnings appear as Nexo guidance only when present, alongside contextual Nexo actions; absent advice creates no filler or invented answer. The PSN objective has a separate target icon. Hidden pending trophies require one explicit game-wide reveal, persisted by the authenticated backend per account and catalog game, loaded through `/api/me`; failed saves do not reveal anything. The server's `todaysFocus` can highlight a suggestion without changing its ranking. Guide order is advisory and never claims the player's confirmed location or a guaranteed unlock sequence.
The campaign map is the opening view over game artwork: route origin and actual platinum destination remain reachable, with pending milestones on a connected path and arrow navigation through fixed pages. A distinct suggested marker is separate from the selected inspection point; neither claims the save location. “Along the way” exposes known ongoing objectives attached to the route. Selecting any milestone updates the actionable guidance immediately below the map, retaining reviewed extended advice and recovery under “How to do it and what to watch for”. The base-game track ends at its platinum; additional trophies remain reachable through the objective selector on a separate track. Full-reference route controls and direct objective jumps are secondary; confirmed history remains independently accessible. Completed unlocks are never removed from the account or treated as proof of chronological position. Personal-progress context, comparison/spoiler options and provenance remain accessible; comparison always carries a visible notice.

3. Collapsible session route (`PlanCard` scoped by PSN list id). One active route per account; making another replaces the previous route and the UI states this. Reviewed Requiem/Odyssey editions select from the shared journey candidates, reserve time blocks rather than claiming duration estimates, preserve metadata in flexible steps and re-evaluate eligibility after unlocks. Missing coverage fails without replacing the previous plan; hidden objectives respect account preferences. Unreviewed editions retain the existing generator.
4. `GameColumns` switches between `TrophyList` and the full `Guide` on the journey, preserving filters, sorting, help, guide numbers, sources, nested tabs and pending states. No invented trophy completion.
5. Similar games remain reachable through the sticky section navigation when available.

Nexo links open a dialog above the mounted journey, preserving scroll, filters and expanded sections. Mobile uses the available viewport with safe areas. Returning closes the dialog. Direct `/nexo` still opens the general conversation page and history.

## Game not owned

Preserve the scene backdrop, immersive hero with catalog facts, interest action and store link, guide and catalog trophies. Never show personal progress or offer a personal session plan. When catalog trophies exist, show the shared roadmap in explicitly labeled start-preview mode, retaining the catalog game identifier for contextual chat. Missing guide or trophy data is not synthesized. A selected-game plan requires ownership on the server.

## Navigation

Primary destinations are Home, Jornada, Guias, Descobrir and Jogos. Home retains its console and artwork at `/`; `/overview` redirects there. Jogos contains the synced PSN library and the ordered interests and deals, and a separate Goals and achievements tab with the annual target, calendar and earned platinums. `/goals` redirects to `/games?tab=goals`. Profile and guide search remain available. General Nexo, history and settings remain accessible from the account menu.

## States and validation

- Loading uses skeletons, never fabricated progress.
- No current game offers Jogos; failed sync is explicit.
- Missing guide uses the existing queued/researching states and polling stops on unmount.
- Failed plan loading must not invite a replacement as though no route exists.
- Keep pt-BR and English messages, semantic tokens, 44px primary touch targets and reduced-motion support from shared components.
- Stories cover progress, zero progress, guide pending, completion, loading, empty and failure across journey and list containers.
- Run lint, types, coverage, build, ui:check and stories:check. Inspect actual phone and desktop pixels, plus contextual chat, keyboard and return behavior before claiming visual validation.
